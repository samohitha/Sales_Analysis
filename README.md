🧾 Sales Analysis
📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on understanding the overall sales performance of a company by analyzing key areas such as revenue by region, month, year, top customers, and products. The goal is to explore how different factors affect total revenue and to identify patterns that can help in making better business decisions.

🎯 Objectives

Analyze sales trends across different months and years

Compare sales performance by region

Identify top customers based on revenue

Find top-selling products and their contribution to total revenue

Visualize insights using interactive dashboards

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

Power BI – for creating dashboards and visual reports

SQL / MySQL – for querying and cleaning raw data
